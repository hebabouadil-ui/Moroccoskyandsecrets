— About us

A Marrakech-born family agency.

Morocco Skies & Secrets is a Marrakech-based travel and ballooning company. We started with a single balloon over the palmeraie and grew into a full agency curating flights, city circuits and cultural experiences across the Kingdom.

All our pilots are licensed by the Moroccan Civil Aviation Authority, our vehicles are regularly serviced, and every itinerary is designed by people who actually live here.
